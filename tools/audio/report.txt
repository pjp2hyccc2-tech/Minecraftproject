Loop seams: the click measure at the wrap point compared with the same measure at 40 interior points
of the file; a seam ranked above 98% of them with hf > 3 whose HF energy is also above the file's typical
level is flagged CLICK? (in a silent gap the local ratio only measures codec noise).
Columns: peak = sample peak dBFS, TP = true peak dBTP (4x oversampled), LUFS = BS.1770 integrated,
Mmax = max momentary (400 ms) loudness, crest = peak - RMS, cent = spectral centroid Hz,
rough = RMS amplitude-modulation depth in the 30-150 Hz roughness band (noise ~0.07-0.1,
clean tones ~0, screams/AM 0.2+).  Loop seam (decoded file, wrap end->start): hf = 2nd-difference
energy across the seam / median of the windows just before the end and just after the start;
step = |x[0]-x[-1]| / median local sample step.  ~1 = as smooth as the surrounding material, a click
would show as >> 3.  binaural = detected carrier pairs (a tone in one ear only, its partner 2-10 Hz
away in the other ear); faint pairs buried in the wind / tunnel noise are not always detected.
